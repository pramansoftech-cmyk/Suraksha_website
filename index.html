Project 01

150 KM Demonstration Corridor

SURAKSHA™ includes a proposed 150 km demonstration corridor near NH-44, designed to demonstrate an integrated approach to health, safety, technology and partnerships for vulnerable mobile populations.

The demonstration corridor will provide an opportunity to understand needs on the ground, test practical interventions, generate evidence and identify approaches that can be adapted and scaled.

  • Screen & Identify: Understand health needs and risks.
  • Intervene & Refer: Connect people with appropriate support.
  • Learn & Scale: Use evidence to improve and expand the model.

Project 03

Mining & Infrastructure Workforces

India's logistics networks and mining operations connect communities and drive economic growth. Yet many vulnerable mobile populations face barriers to timely healthcare and emergency support.

SURAKSHA™ seeks to bring health and safety closer to where people live, work and travel—focusing heavily on commercial vehicle drivers, construction workers, and high-risk mining operators.
