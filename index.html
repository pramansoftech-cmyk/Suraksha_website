The Core Initiative

SURAKSHA™ Mission 1 Million

Highlighted at the Conclave, Mission 1 Million is a nationwide movement to improve healthcare access for one million highway users. It serves as India's first AI-enabled Highway Health, Safety & Workforce Wellbeing Demonstration Corridor.

Predictive AI Screening

Identifying risks before they become emergencies.

Digital Teleconsultation

Connecting drivers to qualified doctors anywhere, anytime.

Emergency Response

Rapid ambulance deployment and trauma care networking.

Community Wellness

Health education and nutrition support at rest stops.
